    Archiving

    Summarize
    Archive
    Keep loaded

    Tabs you haven't opened for a while first unload their pages and shrink to one line: which site, how long ago. Opening one loads it again. Later they close and leave the sidebar, which offers Undo until you've seen it and hide jbrow. Find them under Archived or by typing in the address bar. Kept and pinned tabs never archive. Their pages unload too, keeping their full row, unless Keep loaded is on for them; pinned tabs kept loaded also load when jbrow starts, so they're ready when you click. A folder in Kept can catch tabs by address before they archive: right-click the folder and choose Auto-file rules.

    Archived

    Return or a double-click opens a tab, Restore puts it back in the sidebar, both with its back and forward history. ⌘-click, Space or the box by a tab selects it. Archived tabs are forgotten after 90 days.

    Apps

    ⌥⇧Space shows only the tabs of the app you were in, and the sidebar groups each app's tabs under its name. Pick the profile an app's new tabs open in; Auto uses the tab you were on. Right-click an app to link it with another, so they share one set of tabs.

    Restyling

    The wand in the toolbar opens restyling in the sidebar: describe how the site you're on should look, and the agent writes a stylesheet that applies to every page of that site.
